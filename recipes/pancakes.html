Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are a popular breakfast dish. They are made from flour, milk, and eggs. Sugar can be added for sweetness. They are cooked in a pan. They are usually served warm.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Prepare the batter
  2. Mix ingredients
  3. Heat the pan
  4. Add butter
  5. Pour the batter
  6. Cook both sides
