Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a traditional Italian pasta dish. It consists of layers of pasta, meat sauce, and béchamel sauce. The layers are topped with melted cheese. It is baked in the oven until golden and bubbling. It is usually served hot as a main course.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Bechamel sauce

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce
  2. Prepare bechamel
  3. Layer
  4. Add cheese
  5. Bake
