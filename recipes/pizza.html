Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish. It is made from dough, tomato sauce, and cheese. Different toppings can be added. It is baked in the oven. It is usually served hot.

Ingredients

  • Water
  • Yeast
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Flour

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough
  2. Prepare sauce
  3. Roll dough
  4. Add sauce
  5. Add cheese
  6. Bake
